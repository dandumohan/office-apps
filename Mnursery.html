👤 Beneficiary Details & Sanction Details

Optional – save the batch first, then add or update these any time.

* Required: From/To Date and at least one Nursery / Scheme line. Beneficiaries are optional; for each beneficiary you add the name, mobile (10 digits), no. of seedlings and distribution date. Aadhar, if given, must be 12 digits. Aadhar and mobile numbers must be unique across beneficiaries. Sanction Quantity is filled automatically (the beneficiary’s seedlings, at most 200); tick Exempted to edit it, with a note on why / on what basis. In Select Seedlings a beneficiary can receive only seedlings of the sanction scheme, up to the Sanction Quantity. A new Sanction ID is generated automatically; each beneficiary needs a different Sanction ID within the batch. Sanction Quantity cannot exceed the beneficiary’s seedlings, Distribution Date must fall within the batch dates, and Free / Cost No. are filled from the Sanction Quantity (Cost when “Sanctioned For” is Cost, otherwise Free).

✅ Batch saved. Go to step 2 to select the seedlings for it.
